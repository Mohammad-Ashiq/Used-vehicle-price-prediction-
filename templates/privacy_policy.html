Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ReVahan ("we", "our", or "us"). We provide an Artificial Intelligence-powered Used Vehicle Price Prediction System for Cars, Bikes, Commercial Lorries, and Auto Rickshaws across India. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our web application, mobile application, and the choices you have associated with that data.

2. Information Collection and Use

While using ReVahan, we may ask you to provide vehicle specifications to compute valuation estimates, including:

  • Vehicle Category, Brand, Model, and Variant
  • Year of Manufacture, Kilometers Driven, and Fuel Type
  • Transmission Type and State/Region of Registration

We do not collect sensitive personal data such as financial account numbers, Aadhaar numbers, or biometric information.

3. Device & Usage Data

When you access ReVahan via a mobile device or web browser, we may collect certain information automatically, including the type of mobile device you use, mobile operating system, browser type, and diagnostic data to improve application performance.

4. Security of Data

The security of your data is important to us. All communications between your client device and our servers are encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, but we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect all data.

5. Children's Privacy

Our service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
