ReVahan – Used Vehicle Price Prediction System

A complete Full-Stack Data Science & Web Engineering application designed to eliminate market opacity in the Indian pre-owned vehicle ecosystem.

Problem Statement & Vision

The used vehicle market in India is predominantly unorganized, characterized by high price volatility, asymmetrical dealer information, and arbitrary valuation markups. Buyers frequently overpay while genuine sellers struggle to establish credible fair market prices.

This project delivers an end-to-end Machine Learning solution that analyzes verified vehicle parameters—including manufacturing age, odometer mileage wear, fuel dynamics, commercial load capacity, ownership titles, and mechanical conditions—to predict an objective, data-backed fair resale value in Indian Rupees (₹).

Machine Learning Engine

We employ Scikit-Learn's Random Forest Regressor, an ensemble bagging technique that constructs 120 randomized decision trees during training. Random Forest was selected over linear regression and individual decision trees because:

  • Non-Linear Dynamics: Accurately models non-linear vehicle depreciation curves across multi-year cycles.
  • Robust to Overfitting: Aggregation (bagging) of 120 trees drastically reduces model variance and error.
  • Categorical Interactions: Seamlessly handles interactions between brands, fuel types, and transmission modes.
