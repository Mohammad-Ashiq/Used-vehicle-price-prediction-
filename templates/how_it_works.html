Technical Walkthrough

How The Machine Learning Pipeline Works

A step-by-step breakdown of data collection, preprocessing, model architecture, and inference calculation.

1

Data Collection & Ingestion

The system maintains independent, category-specific datasets (cars.csv, bikes.csv, lorries.csv, autos.csv) reflecting realistic Indian automotive resale transactions across Tier-1 and Tier-2 cities.

Key Captured Attributes: Manufacturing Year, Odometer Kilometers, Fuel Type, Engine Displacement/Tonnage, Condition Rating, and Ownership Title.
2

Feature Engineering & Preprocessing

Raw inputs undergo statistical preparation in ml/preprocess.py before model training and inference:

  • Age Transformation: Converts manufacturing year into active lifecycle age: Age = 2026 - Year.
  • Numerical Standardization: Continuous values (age, km, cc, payload) are centered using StandardScaler.
  • Categorical Encoding: Multi-class variables (brand, model, fuel, condition, city) are converted into sparse binary matrices using OneHotEncoder(handle_unknown='ignore').
3

Random Forest Ensemble Training

Our training pipeline utilizes an ensemble of 120 regression trees. Each individual tree is trained on a bootstrap sample of data with randomized feature splits.

Ensemble Prediction Formula: \(\hat{y} = \frac{1}{B} \sum_{b=1}^{B} T_b(x)\) where \(B = 120\) trees, eliminating individual tree noise and bias.
4

Confidence Range & Explainable AI

Rather than providing a single arbitrary number, our system computes:

  • Point Valuation: Fair market expected resale price in Indian Rupees (₹).
  • Confidence Interval: Negotiation spread (price_min to price_max) calculated from root mean squared error.
  • Impact Factor Grading: Transparent explanations highlighting why the price shifted based on condition or mileage.

Viva Voce & Review Q&A Guide (BCA Final Year)

Vehicle depreciation is non-linear—a car depreciates much faster in its first 2-3 years (~15-20%) and then stabilizes (~7-9% annually). Linear regression cannot capture steep exponential drops or complex interactions (e.g. diesel engines holding higher value in commercial lorries but lower in small hatchbacks). Random Forest handles non-linear relationships and interactions without requiring manual polynomial transformations.

Overfitting is avoided through: 1. Random Forest ensemble bagging (averaging 120 trees). 2. Limiting max_depth=16 and setting min_samples_split=4 and min_samples_leaf=2. 3. Evaluating exclusively on unseen 20% test splits using Scikit-Learn's train_test_split.

The trained Scikit-Learn pipelines (including the preprocessors and decision trees) are serialized into binary pickle files using joblib.dump() in the models/ directory. When Flask launches, ml/predict.py deserializes the models into an in-memory dictionary cache, ensuring sub-50ms prediction latency.
