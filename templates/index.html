How The Prediction Engine Works

Our intelligent Machine Learning architecture converts raw vehicle specifications into a statistically validated fair market valuation.

1
Input Specifications

Enter brand, model, manufacturing year, kilometers driven, fuel type, vehicle condition, and title history.

2
Feature Engineering

Raw parameters are processed, missing values handled, numerical features scaled, and categorical variables One-Hot encoded.

3
Random Forest Inference

An ensemble of 120 decision trees aggregates non-linear market patterns and depreciation curves to predict price.

4
Appraisal & PDF Report

Receive fair resale value in ₹ (INR), negotiation price window, factor breakdown, and downloadable PDF certificate.
